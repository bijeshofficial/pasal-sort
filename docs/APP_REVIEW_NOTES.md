# App Review notes (Pasal Sort)

Saved in App Store Connect → App Review Information → Notes for version
1.0.0. If Apple asks for more information (Guideline 2.1), reply with this
text plus a screen recording made on a real iPhone from app launch.

```
1. Purpose and audience
Pasal Sort: Sort & Renovate is a single-player casual puzzle game for all ages (4+). The player sorts colourful wrapped candies into glass jars: tap a jar, then tap another, and the top candies pour across. A jar filled with one kind of candy is sealed; sorting every jar wins the level. Each win earns a star, and stars are spent on renovation tasks that restore a family sweet shop ("pasal") in Kathmandu, room by room, with a short story. The game is relaxing, has no timers, and plays offline.

2. Setup and main features
No account, login or sign-up. Install, open and play. Levels 1-3 have guided tutorials (a hand shows what to tap), and the first renovation task is guided too.
- Home: the shop being renovated. PLAY starts the next level. Tasks spends stars on renovation. Buttons at the sides open Areas, Daily rewards, Missions, the sticker Album, the Daily Challenge and the Star chest.
- In a level: tap jars to pour candies. Boosters at the bottom (Undo, Extra Jar, Shuffle, Haat Helper) help when stuck.
- Shop (left tab): boosters, lives, sticker packs and cosmetic jar and theme styles, bought with in-game coins earned by playing. A "Watch" button gives free daily coins for an optional rewarded ad.
- Profile (right tab): an editable display name and avatar (stored only on the device), stats and achievements.
- Settings (gear, top right): sound, music, vibration, language (English or Nepali), colour-blind candy markers, text size, privacy policy, ad privacy options and credits.
All content is unlocked by playing. Nothing requires payment, and there are no in-app purchases.

3. External services
- Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK): optional rewarded ads only. An ad plays only when the player taps a "Watch" button for a bonus (free daily coins, an extra life, extra moves, a booster, or doubled coins). There are no banner or interstitial ads and no ads between levels.
- Google User Messaging Platform: the ad consent form where required (EEA, UK, Switzerland) and the IDFA explainer shown before Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt.
The app has no user accounts, no in-app purchases, no payment processing, no AI services and no server of its own. Progress is saved only on the device. The game is fully playable offline.

4. Regional differences
The app works the same in all regions. The only difference is legally required: players in the EEA, UK and Switzerland see Google's ad consent form. The app is in English and Nepali (switch in Settings).

5. Regulated industry / third-party material
Not applicable. The app is not in a regulated industry. All art, sound and text were created for the game; the shop, its family and the neighbours are fictional.

User-generated content: none is shared between players (no chat, multiplayer or public profiles). The display name is stored only on the device.
```
